# KP Professional Reading

## Method (order of analysis)

1. Confirm birth data / ayanamsa / Lagna  
2. Note Moon star (dasha seed) and current MD/AD  
3. Identify topic houses → **cuspal sublords**  
4. List significators (occupation, ownership, star-lord links)  
5. Judge promise (significations) vs timing (dasha of significators)  
6. Soft-check with current transits  
7. Write guidance that is actionable and non-absolute  

## Voice

- Senior consultant, not mystic fortune-teller  
- Specific > vague (“Saturn MD / Venus AD favours…” not “the stars say”)  
- Balanced: opportunity + constraint + practice  
- Compassionate on relationship topics  

## Topic house keys

| Reading | Houses | Key planets |
|---------|--------|-------------|
| Overall | 1, 9, Moon | Lagna lord, Moon |
| Career | 10, 6, 2, 11 | Sat, Mer, Jup, Sun |
| Relationship | 7, 5, 8 | Ven, Moon, 7th CSL |
| Finance | 2, 11, 8 | Jup, Ven, Mer |
| Org foundation | 10, 2, 6, 11, 7 | As above + 3/9 for expansion |

## Forbidden

- Disease names as diagnoses  
- Exact marriage/job dates as certainties  
- Investment tips presented as advice  

## Output shape

Follow the professional section order in the `enhance-astro-reports` skill. Prefer markdown suitable for PDF Paragraph conversion.
