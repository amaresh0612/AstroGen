Prioritize 10th, 6th, 2nd, and 11th cuspal sublords; then significators of those houses.
Use current and upcoming Vimshottari MD/AD for timing of job change, recognition, or skill-building windows.

## Bad vs good claim

Bad: “You will become a CEO in 2027.”

Good: “With 10th CSL Mercury and MD Saturn / AD Mercury active into 2027, professional visibility and structured advancement are favoured—especially in analytical or advisory roles—subject to effort and circumstances.”
